About Me

Hello, I'm Alex Morgan, a visual storyteller based in New York City. With a keen eye for detail and a passion for capturing authentic moments, I create images that evoke emotion and tell compelling stories.

My approach blends technical precision with artistic intuition, whether I'm shooting portraits, landscapes, or commercial projects. I believe in finding beauty in the unexpected and revealing the extraordinary in the everyday.
